Hi BoBers,

Not too much to report since the last entry. I'm finally off the highway on a well graded gravel road. It's been mostly flat with vast fields of dry grass on either side of the road. My right pedal snapped off, so I've been at a campground since Saturday waiting for a replacement. The rest is well needed though because my left knee has still been hurting. I really hope it won't flare up too much over the next couple of weeks. 

The kindness has been incredible too. As I crossed the Tropic of Capricorn, I met two families and they gave me a massive breakfast, a beer, a pile of snacks and even some cash! I hailed down another couple for some water, who also gave me a bunch of snacks. I'm incredibly touched. Martin, my host from Windhoek, had some spare pedals lying around and his friend is dropping them off to me on his way home. 

The pedal should arrive late this arvo and I'll be able to continue south on my way to Oranjemund. By next week I should be across the border to South Africa!

Too right,
J&BoB
