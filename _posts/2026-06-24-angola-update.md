Hi BoBers,

Sorry for the long delay. I was quite sick in Equatorial Guinea and Gabon, had some bike troubles in Congo and had a lot of big riding days in Angola.

I just crossed the border from Angola into Namibia! It's the second-last country of the trip and a gradual reintroduction into western life. There's clean water from the taps, people speak English and I can buy peanut butter from the supermarket. I think I will eventually miss the challenge and chaos of Central Africa, but for the moment I'm enjoying the hell out of a red-velvet cake that I paid for by card.

Instead of trying in vain to catch up, I'm just going to continue from Namibia and write about what was in-between after I arrive in Cape Town. A lot happened in Equatorial Guinea and Gabon, so I promise it will be worth the wait.

Here are some pictures of the last few days of riding in Angola, where there are still some remnants of military equipment.

Comfortably,
J&BoB
