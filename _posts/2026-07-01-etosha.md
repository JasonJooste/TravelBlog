Hi BoBers,

Namibia has been a big change. Suddenly, I have all kinds of luxuries available. It's been nice to indulge a little but also a little frustrating, because at times I feel like I'm being funnelled into the tourist pipeline here. Locals either want to make money, or just feel like these are my standards as a foreigner. With so many people splashing cash here on short holidays, it's hard not to feel cheap when I argue over prices. Though, I also don't enjoy being charged three times the price. 

Either way, I splashed out a little myself. I hired a car to visit the Etosha game reserve, which is quite famous in Southern Africa. It was really cool to see some animals up close and in person for the first time! Still, after two days of driving around in a car I was keen to reunite with BoB and get in closer contact with the environment again. 

Just over a month to go before I arrive in Cape Town and a lot of ks to do, so it'll be head down, tail up for a while again. Next stop is Windhoek, where I can get some parts for my bike and stay with a local host that I found online.

Reunited,
J&BoB
